2026 rewrite

The whole set was rebuilt from the original Evernote exports into clean, semantic HTML. The wording of the notes is unchanged apart from a short list of typo and notation fixes. Both the structural changes and every content fix are recorded in full in CHANGELOG.md, alongside these files.

The headline points:

  • Every page is now valid HTML5 with real headings, properly nested lists, figures and code blocks — no <font> tags, no inline styling, no unclosed elements.
  • Chapter 10 is included again, and the 167 diagrams that were missing from the source folders have been restored, so all 383 images resolve locally.
  • Each chapter carries an "on this page" contents list and previous/next navigation.
  • The pages are readable on a phone, in dark mode, and in print.
